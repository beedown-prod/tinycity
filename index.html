Getting started New players

Tiny City is an idle tycoon: you build a little city that earns money on its own, upgrade it, and keep growing it - even while you watch events or ride a hoverboard.

  1. Claim a plotWalk onto a free plot and claim it. You start with the Farm at level 1 and $50.
  2. Upgrade the FarmPress the upgrade sign in front of a building. Every level changes the model and earns more money per second.
  3. Unlock new buildingsYour city level is all your building levels added up. New buildings open at city level 3, 6, 10, 15 and onwards.
  4. Catch the eventsAbout twice an hour a city event starts and everyone earns more - from x1.5 on a Sunny Day to x15 during a System Glitch.
  5. RebirthWhen you can afford it, rebirth: you start over, but earn +50% forever and every building gets 2 new levels.
  6. Collect everythingEarn titles (each one boosts your income), spin the Daily Spin, race your hoverboard and unlock skins and styles.

How money works The formula

Every second, each building pays out its level's income. Then everything is multiplied together. Multipliers multiply each other, so stacking them grows fast.

BuildingsSum of incomes× Passes1.5 · 1.5× Rebirths1 + 0.5 R× Eventx1.5 - x50× Titles1 + boosts× Skills1 + Midas= You earn$ per second

What each part means

  • Buildings: the income of every building at its current level, added up (see the Buildings table).
  • Passes: Money Boost x1.5 and VIP x1.5. Both together give x2.25.
  • Rebirths: +50% per rebirth, added up. 15 rebirths = x8.5. Ascensions keep it.
  • Event: while a city event runs, the whole server earns its multiplier (x1 when nothing is on).
  • Titles: the boosts of ALL your titles add up, e.g. +5% +8% +12% = x1.25. CHAMPION gives +2% per rebirth, TRUE CHAMPION +40%.
  • Skills: Golden Touch I, II and III add +10%, +15% and +25% (x1.5 with all three).

Prices of the next level

Level n of a small building costs BaseCost × 1.9n-1 and earns BaseIncome × 1.7n-1. Rebirth levels continue at cost x1.62 and income x1.25 per level (Farm x1.32 / x1.15). Ascension levels: cost x1.12, income x1.15.

A rebirth costs $400M × 2.3R × 1.055R(R-1)/2, where R is the rebirths you already have. Money is capped at about $9 Quadrillion.

How a rebirth works

Pay the price, and your money and every building go back to the start (Farm level 1). In return you get:

  • +50% income forever (they add up: 2 rebirths = x2.0, 15 = x8.5)
  • +2 levels on every building, in a new colour every rebirth
  • The 15th rebirth is the CHAMPION rebirth: heavenly white-and-gold buildings

Rough play time without passes: first rebirth ~35 min, rebirths 2-5 ~30 min each, rebirth 9 ~3 h, rebirth 14 ~88 h. All 15 take around 380 hours.

Ascension Late game

After all 15 rebirths the ASCENSION button appears. Ascending keeps your 15 rebirths and their x8.5 income, resets your money and buildings, and gives 2 skill points plus 2 cosmic levels on every building.

To ascend you need

  • 15 rebirths
  • Every building at its very last level (after the first ascension, the 6 new buildings too)
  • The money price and the hours played in the table

Your first ascension opens the land behind your plot: a starlit grand plaza with 6 new buildings - Movie Studio, Stadium, Theme Park, Airport, Fusion Plant and Space Port.

Skill tree 12 skills

Every ascension gives 2 skill points (20 in total). The whole galaxy costs 17, so you can learn everything. A skill needs the one before it.

City events Everyone earns more

After an event there are at least 8 quiet minutes, then a new one usually starts within about 20 minutes - roughly two events an hour. While one is on, the whole server earns the multiplier.

Daily spin Free every 24 h

One free spin a day with the wheel button. Money prizes are minutes of your own income, so they grow with your city. The Lucky Star skill doubles them.

Plot skins Change your whole plot

Pick a skin in INVENTORY > SKINS. Recolour skins tint your normal city; the big skins replace every building with all-new models that change on every upgrade.

Hoverboards Press F to ride

Every board is just as fast - they only look different. The COMET branch of the skill tree makes all of them faster.

UI styles Change every menu

Styles change the look of every screen. Pick one in INVENTORY > STYLES.

Titles Income boosts

Titles are earned by playing and show above your head and in chat. Almost every title also adds a permanent income boost, and the boosts add up. Earn all 47 playable ones for the BOUNDLESS title (+75%), which also unlocks the Boundless plot, board and style.

Hoverboard race Turbo Tess

How to race

Talk to TURBO TESS, the NPC in the racing helmet next to the main road. Race alone, or against a rival who is sometimes one of your Roblox friends. The course runs through rings along the ring of roads around the city; a lap takes about 1 to 1.5 minutes.

Rewards

  • Finish a lap: 30 seconds of your income
  • New personal best: +30 seconds of income
  • Beat the rival: +75 seconds of income

Racing a lot in 20 minutes slowly lowers the reward (never under 40%); it wears off by itself. Race titles: RACER, QUICK RIDER (under 1:35), SPEED DEMON (under 1:12), ROAD KING (under 1:00) and RIVAL CRUSHER (beat 10 rivals).

Shop Robux

Passes and items in the SHOP. Buying anything also earns the PAY TO WIN title. Prices are shown in the game.

!

Prices may vary. Robux prices can be different depending on whether a player has Roblox Premium and on their country (Roblox can set different prices per region). The price shown in the game when you buy is always the one you pay.

Stormforge serials LIMITED MYTHIC #1 - #100

The first 100 Stormforge Bundles that are bought get a serial number. The game's owner has #0. After #100 the bundle still works, just without a number.

  • Black and white edition: a serial copy turns the plot, the fights, the UI style, the hoverboard and its INVENTORY cards black and white. Prefer the colours? Switch between B&W and COLOUR any time in SETTINGS > SERIAL LOOK - your number stays yours either way.
  • Your number on your cosmetics: with the black and white look it floats over the upgrade altar of your main building (the Farm in the middle), sits on a plate on your hoverboard and on your HUD with the style. With the normal colours the number is hidden there. The cards in your INVENTORY always show it.
  • A black and white cinematic: when you get a serial, the unlock cutscene plays in black and white and its title card says which number you got.
  • Only for real purchases: buying the bundle, or another player buying it as a gift for you. Gifts from the owner panel never come with a number.
  • The SHOP shows how many numbered copies are left.
  • The end card of the cinematic lists what you got - PLOT SKIN, UI STYLE, HOVERBOARD - and your serial number.
